op-geth has served as the primary execution client for OP Stack chains since the beginning. As the ecosystem matures, we are transitioning full execution client support to **op-reth**, which will be the only supported execution client starting with the Glamsterdam hardfork.

<Warning>
  op-geth will reach end-of-support in **May 2026**. The Glamsterdam hardfork will not include op-geth support. Begin migrating to op-reth immediately.
</Warning>

## What this means

- **op-geth is supported through May 2026.** Security patches and critical bug fixes will continue to be issued during this window.
- **Glamsterdam breaks op-geth compatibility.** The upcoming Glamsterdam hardfork will only be supported on op-reth. Chains still running op-geth at activation will not be able to follow the canonical chain.
- **op-reth is production-ready.** op-reth has been battle-tested on mainnet chains and is ready for production use today.
- **The fault proof system is moving to Kona.** The proof program is transitioning from op-program to kona-cannon. This is already underway and will complete ahead of the Glamsterdam activation.

## Action required

**All node and chain operators must migrate to op-reth before the Glamsterdam hardfork activation.**

The following chains are specifically called out to begin syncing op-reth nodes immediately:

- OPW
- WORLD
- INK
- Soneium
- Unichain

<Info>
  Do not wait until Glamsterdam is scheduled. Start syncing op-reth nodes now to allow time for validation before the hardfork window.
</Info>

### Migration steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy an op-reth node alongside your existing op-geth node">
    Run op-reth in sync mode without directing production traffic to it. This lets you validate correctness before cutover.

    Refer to the [op-reth documentation](https://reth.rs/run/op-stack) for installation and configuration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Validate sync and output consistency">
    Compare block hashes, state roots, and RPC outputs between your op-geth and op-reth nodes over a meaningful window to confirm they agree.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Migrate production traffic to op-reth">
    Once validated, switch your sequencer, batcher, and proposer to point to op-reth as the execution client.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decommission op-geth">
    After successful cutover and a stabilization period, decommission your op-geth nodes. No further updates will be issued for op-geth after May 2026.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Proof system transition

In parallel with the execution client migration, the fault proof program is moving from **op-program** to **kona-cannon**. This transition is in progress and does not require separate action from most operators—Optimism will handle the onchain prestate updates for managed chains. If you operate a permissionless fault proof chain, watch for a separate notice with specific steps.
